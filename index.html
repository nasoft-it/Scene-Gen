About SceneGen

SceneGen helps creators discover reusable video, image, and music assets from sources across the web, so you spend less time hunting for footage and more time editing.

SceneGen doesn't generate media and doesn't claim ownership over anything it surfaces. Every result links back to where it came from, along with whatever license information that source publishes.

SceneGen helps locate media with reusable licenses. Always verify the original source's current license and usage terms before publishing.
